Privacy

Very little, and only
with your permission.

This site describes a tool whose whole purpose is keeping sensitive text off other people’s servers. Its own analytics are held to the same standard: nothing is measured until you allow it, and everything that is measured fits on this page.

Collected

Only after you allow analytics

Analytics are provided by PostHog, which processes this data on our behalf. The library starts switched off; it begins collecting only when you choose “Allow analytics” in the banner.

01

Pageviews & clicks

Which pages you visit, which links and buttons you use, and when you leave a page. That is how we learn whether anyone reads the benchmarks.

PostHog
02

Where you came from

The site that referred you, and any UTM campaign tags on the link you followed to get here.

PostHog
03

Browser & device

Browser, operating system, screen size and language — the standard properties the analytics library attaches to each event.

PostHog
04

IP address, anonymized

Your IP address reaches PostHog with each event, and our project is configured to anonymize it. We do not store or use your raw IP address.

Anonymized

Never collected

By design, not by promise
—

No session recording

Session replay is disabled in the snippet on every page. Nobody can watch your visit back.

Off
—

Nothing from the demo

Automatic capture is switched off on the demo page and the editor is marked no-capture. What you type or paste there stays in your browser — it is never sent to PostHog, or anywhere.

Never sent
—

No advertising, no cross-site tracking

There are no ad networks, no social-media pixels and no third-party trackers on this site. The analytics identifier is first-party to this site and is not used to follow you across the web.

None
—

No sale or sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. That is the California wording, and it is simply true: analytics data goes to PostHog, our service provider, and nowhere else.

Not sold

Hosting & signals

The parts around the edges
—

Served by GitHub Pages

This site is static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses in its server logs; that logging is GitHub’s, it is outside our control, and it is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Hosting
—

Signals honored

If your browser sends Do Not Track, the analytics library respects it. If it sends Global Privacy Control, this site treats that as a “no”: capturing stays off and no banner is shown.

DNT · GPC

Your rights

And how to use them
—

In Europe

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be erased, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Because the analytics are anonymous and consent-gated, there is usually nothing identifiable to retrieve — but the rights are yours, and the contact below works.

GDPR
—

In California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to have it deleted, and to opt out of any sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of beyond declining analytics — which the banner and the footer both let you do.

CCPA / CPRA
—

Contact

Privacy questions and requests — access, erasure, anything else — go through this site’s repository. Open an issue and it will be answered there.
