First

It runs in this tab

The model executes on your machine, in the browser. There is no redaction endpoint to send text to — the page works with the network cable pulled.

Second

It downloads once

The 11 MB model is fetched a single time and cached by your browser. After that, every redaction is local arithmetic, at the speeds in the tables.

Third

Closing the tab ends it

Your text lives only in this page’s memory. Close the tab and it is gone — there is no account, no history, and nothing on a server to delete.
