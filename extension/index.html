The Chrome extension

For the words in your browser.

Most text leaves through a browser tab — pasted into a form, a ticket, a chat with an AI. The extension redacts it before it goes: a page you are reading, a passage you are about to paste, a file you are about to attach. The redaction happens in the tab; there is no server round-trip.

How it works

Three ways in, one way out
01

Pick the text

Redact the page you are on, paste text into the extension, or drop in a file. The same 11 MB model and rules engine from the SDKs do the finding.

Step one
02

Certain findings are replaced

Names, dates, ID numbers and the rest are redacted in place, in the tab, before the text is copied, submitted or attached anywhere.

Step two
03

Borderline findings wait for you

A detection near the confidence threshold is neither silently kept nor silently removed. It is held in the review bucket, with its context, for your call.

In-tab processing
